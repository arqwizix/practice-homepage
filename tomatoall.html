Agridera Fresh Market Eggplants

*****

Agridera aims to be one of the world's leading companies in the breeding, production, and marketing of eggplant seeds. We invest intensively in research and development to provide solutions to growers and consumers in numerous countries around the world. In particular, we breed improved varieties adapted to different markets globally.

Currently, emphasis is placed on combining attractive fruit shape, deep purple color, glossiness, strong plant vigor and stable production performance. Comprehensive efforts are being undertaken to develop eggplant varieties suited for fresh market use and commercial cultivation.

In terms of horticultural traits, breeding is for uniformity, firmness, glossy skin, attractive shape, shelf life and dependable fruit set. Combinations of quality features and disease resistance are sought in a variety of types for different market needs.

Agridera eggplant breeding programs are focusing on reliable market varieties with attractive fruit quality and stable field performance.
